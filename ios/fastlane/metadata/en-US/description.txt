Check-Point HRM is attendance and HR for companies that want to know who's in, where, and on time — without spreadsheets or fingerprint machines.

CHECK IN WITH ONE TAP
Employees check in and out from their phone. The app shows today's shift, work hours, and whether they're working from the office or remotely.

GEOFENCED ATTENDANCE
Draw each branch on the map and set exactly where check-ins are allowed. Office, remote, or hybrid — every employee follows the right rule.

A LIVE TEAM DASHBOARD
Admins see total staff, who's present today, who's absent, and pending requests — on one screen, with a weekly attendance chart.

EVERY CHECK-IN, ON RECORD
Search attendance by employee, department, or status. Filter by today, 30, 90, or 180 days. Correct a record when something goes wrong.

LEAVES, OVERTIME, AND WORK-LOCATION REQUESTS
Employees request leave and see their remaining days, log overtime, and ask to change work location. Supervisors and admins approve from the app.

FIELD VISITS AND CUSTOMERS
Plan client visits, add who's going with you, and log the outcome when it's done. Keep branches, sites, and customers in one place.

ROLES FOR EVERYONE
Employee, Supervisor, and Admin each get the screens they need. Switch roles from the home screen if you have more than one.
